# Min's Speed-Dating Interview Guide — ContextLens (Checkpoint 2)

**Who:** Min Kim — 2 interviews required (target users or peers)
**Format:** 15 minutes each, speed-dating style — show the concept fast, react to storyboards, capture quotes
**Goal:** Find where our assumptions break: what would employees actually trust, ignore, or refuse?

---

## Before you start

- Pick 2 people: ideal = someone who's worked a service job (barista, retail, front desk, event staff) or a classmate. Peers are explicitly allowed.
- Bring: this script + the 3 storyboard frames (in `min_kim_validation.md`, section 3) on your phone/laptop.
- Ask permission: "Mind if I take notes? No recording, no real names in the notes."

## The 15-minute script

**0:00–2:00 — Setup (no leading)**
> "I'm on a team building ContextLens — glasses that recognize customers who opted in, and show the employee a short reminder like a name or usual order. Nothing built yet; I want your honest reactions, especially what would bother you."

**2:00–6:00 — Storyboard 1: The café regular (show frame, then ask)**
> "A regular walks in. The glasses recognize them and show: 'Alex Kim — iced espresso. Confirm?' The barista taps confirm."
1. "What would you think if you were the barista?"
2. "What if the glasses were wrong about the order — how bad is that?"
3. "Would you trust it more or less over time?"

**6:00–10:00 — Storyboard 2: The wrong person (show frame, then ask)**
> "The glasses misidentify a new customer as a regular and show the wrong name. The employee greets them wrong."
4. "How would you recover from that as the employee? As the customer?"
5. "What should the glasses do when they're not sure — stay silent, or show a guess?"

**10:00–13:00 — Storyboard 3: The summary (show frame, then ask)**
> "After a chat, the glasses draft a 2-line summary for the store's records. The employee reviews it before saving."
6. "Would you review every summary, or start skipping? When would you stop checking?"
7. "Anything about this that feels creepy or off-limits to you?"

**13:00–15:00 — Wrap**
8. "If you could change one thing about this idea, what would it be?"
9. "Who do you think would hate this the most?"
