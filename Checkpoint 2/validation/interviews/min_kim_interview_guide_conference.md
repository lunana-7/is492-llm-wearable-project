# Min's Speed-Dating Interview #1 — Conference/Event Worker

**Who:** Someone who has worked events, conferences, career fairs, or large-scale venues (staff, volunteer, organizer)
**Format:** 15 minutes, speed-dating style
**Goal:** Learn what event staff would trust, ignore, or refuse about recognition glasses

---

## Storyboard frames (show on your phone/laptop)

**Frame 1 — The recognized attendee.** *Event staff member wearing ContextLens glasses. An attendee approaches; the overlay reads: "Jordan Lee — Northwind Labs, returning attendee. Last spoke about internship panel. Confirm?"*
**Frame 2 — The wrong badge.** *The overlay shows the wrong name. The staffer greets them incorrectly; the attendee looks confused.*
**Frame 3 — The debrief.** *After the conversation, the glasses draft a 2-line summary. The staffer reviews it before it saves to the event CRM.*

---

## The 15-minute script

**0:00–2:00 — Setup**
> "I'm on a team building ContextLens — glasses for event staff that recognize attendees who opted in, and quietly show things like their name and organization. Nothing's built yet; I want your honest reactions, especially what would bother you."
1. "What kind of events have you worked, and how many people do you interact with on a typical shift?"

**2:00–6:00 — Frame 1: the recognized attendee**
2. "If the glasses correctly showed you an attendee's name, org, and your last conversation note — how useful is that, really?"
3. "What attendee info would you actually want to see? What would feel like too much?"
4. "Would you worry about treating opted-in attendees differently from everyone else?"

**6:00–10:00 — Frame 2: the wrong badge**
5. "The glasses misidentify someone and you greet them by the wrong name. How do you recover?"
6. "When the system isn't sure who someone is — should it stay silent, or show its best guess?"
7. "After being wrong once, would you keep trusting it?"

**10:00–13:00 — Frame 3: the debrief summary**
8. "After each chat, the glasses draft a short summary for the event's records, and you review it before saving. Would you actually review each one?"
9. "Anything about the camera or the summaries that feels off-limits to you?"

**13:00–15:00 — Wrap**
10. "If you could change one thing about this idea, what would it be?"
11. "Who at an event would hate this the most — staff, attendees, or organizers?"
