# Interview 1 - Aman
Interviewee: international student(Sneha), ~30-person classes (anonymized)


## Context
Classes of about 30. Only one professor knows her by name; others know her by
face. International student whose name is often mispronounced. Does not use
pronouns.

## Key quotes (verbatim)
1. "Around 30 students, in just 1 class prof knows my name. But they know me by face!"
2. "would be cool but I would want to be able to interact with the prof so it shows they're putting in effort to learn my name"
3. "if the prof isn't able to still pronounce my name after telling them, then the glasses can be used to teach them how to pronounce it"
4. "no I won't be comfortable." (on opting in)
5. "Want complete guarantee that sharing such information won't be used against me in any way and if I were to opt out also, it doesn't go against me"

## What surprised me
She liked the idea ("would be cool") but would still not opt in. She also values
the professor's *effort* to learn her name, which a lookup tool could replace.

## Evidence for / against our assumptions
- Students want to be recognized by name -> **Supports**: being known made her feel "seen" and "valued". But it did not affect her participation.
- Opt-in feels voluntary despite the power gap -> **Mixed**: says she would not hesitate to say no, yet wants a guarantee that opting out is never held against her.
- Minimal info (name, pronunciation, pronouns) is acceptable -> **Narrower than expected**: only "previous questions" is comfortable; "all of them" cross the line (grades, attendance, location, photos, social media, inferred).
- Students accept a camera in the room -> **Contradicts**: "That doesn't seem fair."
- Wrong matches are tolerable if correctable -> **Unclear**: her answer was to show the correct pronunciation.

## Concerns raised
Effort replaced by technology; future attendance or grading use ("sounds scary"); non-opted-in students being captured; uncomfortable overall, "slightly leaning towards only large lecture if needed."

## Info they would / would not share
Would share: previous questions.   Would not share: everything else listed.

## Ideas / requests
- Use the glasses as a pronunciation coach when a professor still struggles.
- Student controls the data; keep it only for the course term.
- To trust it, wants to see a student demonstrate it and hear their honest experience.
- To confirm with her: what she meant by "add in some bias towards students who have opted in."
