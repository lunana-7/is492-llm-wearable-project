# Interview 2 - Aman

Interviewee: international student(Chantrice), 40-60-person classes (anonymized)


## Context
Classes of 40-60. Professors usually know her name because she holds
leadership roles and talks with professors after class. Name is often
mispronounced; she corrects people and doesn't mind.

## Key quotes (verbatim)
1. "I felt recognized for my good work."
2. "But would feel special if they did know my name despite a large class."
3. "if they knew my name, I would be more compelled to ask questions even through informal email because I feel that they would respond with care."
4. "if we become overly dependent on this assistance, it might make our functional memory not as good"
5. "I would be worried if I did not attend class and the tech can verify that."

## What surprised me
She is open to opting in ("whynot!") and sees attendance and participation
tracking as a benefit for accuracy. She also worries that relying on the tool
could weaken memory.

## Evidence for / against our assumptions
- Students want to be recognized by name -> **Supports strongly**: being known felt like trust and recognition and would make her more willing to ask questions.
- Opt-in feels voluntary despite the power gap -> **Not a concern for her**: her decision depends on added obligations, her capacity, and the benefit to both sides.
- Minimal info is acceptable -> **She accepts more**: name, major, class project, previous questions/interactions, notes. Line is location, photos, social media.
- Students accept a camera in the room -> **Conditional**: fine if no personal info is extracted and nothing is stored or made public until consent.
- Wrong matches are tolerable if correctable -> **Unclear**: wants system logic and guardrails reevaluated.

## Concerns raised
Data security and FERPA; who has access to the data; context matters (lecture vs office hours vs hallway); over-reliance on the tool; attendance verification if she skipped.

## Info they would / would not share
Would share: name, major, project, previous questions/interactions, notes.   Would not share: location, photos, social media.

## Ideas / requests
- Clear access controls and consent; secure storage.
- Know exactly who can see the data.
- Explain how the technology works to build interest.
