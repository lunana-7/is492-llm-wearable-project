# Min’s demo cues

**Before class** — Run the [live smoke test](../../app/speech/README.md), prepare one consenting demo profile, and ask the participant to speak a fictional follow-up. Keep the laptop service warmed up. Only show the test roster on screen.

**Slide 4 · Edge case and recovery · 1:45**
Open the participant’s roster entry, hand them the phone, and select **Record follow-up**. Say “I will bring the revised water quality chart to office hours.” Show the generated quote. Ask the student to shorten it to “Bring the water quality chart to office hours” and approve. Show the saved card text, then remove the follow-up to restore the original description. If time permits, discard a second draft and show that the previous card is intact. For a service failure, restore the USB tunnel and retry the request.

**Slide 6 · Safety · 0:45**
“Enrollment and recording are separate choices. Face matching stays on the phone. Only the eight-second audio clip crosses the USB tunnel to our laptop. Whisper transcribes it, then Qwen selects a quote from the transcript. A real quote can still be misheard or taken out of context, so the student edits or discards it before the professor can see it. Removing the profile also removes the saved follow-up.”

**If asked about evidence** — Cite the synthetic live-model run and the automated consent, deletion and outage checks. Identify which phone and glasses the team tested in rehearsal before giving hardware timings or accuracy figures.
